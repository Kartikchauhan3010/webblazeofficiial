How It Works

From idea to live in days

Our streamlined 6-step process means you get a stunning, professional website without the stress. You provide your content and feedback — we handle everything else.

01
Requirement Discussion
We understand your business, goals, target audience, and content. You share your logo, photos, and any references. 50% payment confirmed here.
02
Design Preview
We create a design mockup for your homepage. You approve the look & feel before we build anything. Changes at this stage are easiest.
03
Development
Full website built — all pages, forms, integrations, mobile responsiveness, and SEO setup. You receive a preview link to review.
04
Revisions
You request changes within your revision limit. We refine until it's right. Additional rounds available at ₹500 each if needed.
05
Launch
Final 50% payment. We connect your domain, go live, and submit to Google. Your website is now open for business.
06
Support
Post-launch support covers bug fixes and minor edits within your plan period. Ongoing maintenance available at ₹1,999/month.
